Current Role & Research Focus

I am a Research Investigator at MD Anderson Cancer Center in Houston, TX. My work focuses on bioinformatics and cancer computational biology, including robust pipelines for transcriptomic analysis and discovery of biomarkers that improve clinical decision-making.

My current research emphasizes quantifying endogenous retroviral expression, integrating sequencing modalities, and translating findings into manuscripts, grants, and collaborative studies.
